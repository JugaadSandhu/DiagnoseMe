# A Poem About DiagnoseMe

```
In lines of code and data streams,
Where algorithms chase health dreams,
DiagnoseMe stands, a digital guide,
With Bayes' wisdom as its pride.

Through questions asked of heart and health,
Of lifestyle choices, age, and wealth,
It learns from thousands who came before,
Their patterns stored in CSV lore.

"Have you smoked?" it gently asks,
"Do stairs present you climbing tasks?"
Each answer feeds the probability,
Each truth reveals a possibility.

For hearts that beat with unknown strain,
For sugar levels hard to maintain,
The Naive Bayes begins to see,
What hidden risks might come to be.

Not doctor, no—it makes this clear,
But first alert, a whispered fear,
"Seek help," it says, "consult the wise,
Let trained professionals analyze."

In Python's syntax, clean and bright,
It turns the darkness into light,
A tool of care, a helping hand,
To help us all better understand.

So here's to code that serves the good,
That does for health what software should:
Empower, inform, and gently say,
"Take care of yourself—starting today."

With every prediction, hope takes flight,
As data guides us toward the light.
```

---

*A tribute to the DiagnoseMe repository, where machine learning meets healthcare awareness.*
